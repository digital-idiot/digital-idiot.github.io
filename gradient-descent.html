\[ \begin{aligned} y &= 5\ln(1+x) + 0.65\,x \\ &\quad + \frac{\sin\!\left(0.325\,x^{0.75}\right)}{0.07+0.0035\,x} + \varepsilon \end{aligned} \]

  + sin(0.325·x^0.75)/(0.07+0.0035·x) + ε
\[ x \sim \mathcal{U}(0,\,100), \qquad \varepsilon \sim \mathcal{N}(0,\,\sigma^2) \]

Model

Weights are drawn when you initialise them or press Run.

All three models are trained by the same autograd engine and optimiser settings below. Each keeps its own weights and history, so you can train one, switch, train another and compare. The target‑form model, a·ln(h+x) + b·x + sin(d·xᵉ)/(A+B·x), has exactly the generator's shape: each of its 7 parameters maps to one constant in the target equation (a = 5, h = 1, b = 0.65, d = 0.325, e = 0.75, A = 0.07, B = 0.0035), and nothing is fixed by hand.

Loss

\[ L = \sqrt{\frac{1}{B}\sum_{i=1}^{B}\left(\hat y_i - y_i\right)^2} \]
L = √( (1/B) Σ (ŷᵢ − yᵢ)² )

Optimiser

\[ \theta \leftarrow \theta - \eta\,\nabla_{\theta} L \]
\[ \begin{aligned} m &\leftarrow \beta_1 m + (1-\beta_1)\,g \\ v &\leftarrow \beta_2 v + (1-\beta_2)\,g^2 \\ \theta &\leftarrow \theta - \eta\,\frac{\hat m}{\sqrt{\hat v}+\epsilon} \end{aligned} \]

Polynomial degree

Used by the gradient‑descent polynomial and by the least‑squares (closed‑form) reference. Changing it resets the polynomial model.

Early stopping

Validation RMSE is checked at the end of every epoch. Training stops after “patience” epochs without an improvement larger than min Δ, or at max epochs.

How it works
  • x and y are standardised with the training split's mean and standard deviation so the sigmoids don't saturate on raw x up to 100. RMSE is reported back in y units.
  • Architecture: x → 2 sigmoid → 2 sigmoid → 1 linear output. The output is linear because this is regression.
  • Loss per mini‑batch is L = √(mean e²); its output gradient is eᵢ/(B·L), which is sign(e) when B = 1.
  • Autograd is a scalar reverse‑mode engine in the page; it is gradient‑checked against finite differences.
  • Adam uses bias correction and ε = 1e‑8; its moments reset with the weights.
  • Early stopping checks validation RMSE after each epoch. An improvement must beat the best by more than min Δ.
  • The polynomial uses a Chebyshev basis on x/50 − 1, with degree picked by 5‑fold CV on the training split.

Gradient Update

\[ \theta_{\text{new}} = \theta - \eta\,\frac{\partial L}{\partial \theta} \]
\[ \theta_{\text{new}} = \theta - \eta\,\frac{\hat m}{\sqrt{\hat v}+\epsilon}, \qquad \hat m = \frac{m}{1-\beta_1^{\,t}},\quad \hat v = \frac{v}{1-\beta_2^{\,t}} \]
θ_new = θ − η·∂L/∂θ
